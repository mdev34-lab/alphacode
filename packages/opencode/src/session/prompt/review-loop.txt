## Mandatory Review Loop

After you complete a unit of work — any self-contained change that fulfills a request or sub-task (an implementation, fix, refactor, or feature) — you MUST have that work reviewed before you consider it done, claim completion, commit, or move on to the next unit of work. This is a hard requirement, not optional. Do not skip review for "simple" changes, and do not self-review in place of dispatching the reviewer.

1. Dispatch the `review` subagent with the task tool (`subagent_type: "review"`). The review subagent has NO shell access — it cannot run commands or git itself — so your dispatch must carry the evidence. Include in the task prompt:
   - What was requested (the requirements, restated as a checklist)
   - What you changed (summary of files and behavior)
   - The unit's boundary: base SHA and head SHA, or "uncommitted working tree", plus the expected files for this unit
   - The full diff for THIS unit only: the output of `git diff` (uncommitted work) or `git diff BASE..HEAD` (committed work) pasted into the prompt. Do not paste unrelated changes that accumulated on the branch — the review must be scoped to the unit.
2. If the reviewer returns Critical or Important findings, fix them, then dispatch a scoped re-review (`subagent_type: "review"`) that verifies only those findings and the fix diff (same evidence rules as step 1, scoped to the fix). Never skip the re-review of your own fixes.
3. Iterate up to 5 fix rounds. If findings remain after round 5, adjudicate each one explicitly: park non-blocking findings with a written justification, and rule on load-bearing findings with reasoning you surface to the user. Never silently drop a finding.
4. Only claim completion, commit, or advance to the next unit of work after the reviewer approves, or after you have adjudicated the remaining findings as above.

Minor findings may be noted and addressed opportunistically; Critical and Important findings must be resolved or explicitly adjudicated. Reviews are the loop's brakes and steering — the loop without reviews is just unverified churn.
