End your turn by reporting the outcome. You MUST call this tool exactly once at the end of every reply — it is the only way to complete your turn.

Every user message starts a task, even one that needs no tools. Greetings, questions, and conversational or explanatory answers all end with a finish call; answering without using any tools does not remove the requirement. Producing text is never enough on its own — ending your message alone does not complete the turn. The session keeps running until you call finish.

Pass `result` as a concise summary of what you did or answered. This is a silent protocol signal, not something to announce to the user — do not say that you are finishing; just call it as the last step of your reply.

If the current turn changed files without a completed synchronous review, the first finish call is declined with a review nudge. Running the `review` subagent is strongly recommended; if you have determined that another review pass is unnecessary, call finish again to explicitly skip review and deliver the result, and say so in `result`.

Call it once, after any other tool calls have completed. Do not call it to report partial progress, and do not use it to ask a question — use the question tool for questions and keep working otherwise.

When finish succeeds, it makes a best-effort attempt to close any remaining open todos (pending or in_progress) for this task as cancelled, leaving a clean terminal todo state when possible. This cleanup does not block task completion — if todo closure fails, finish still succeeds and the failure is logged. This is a safety net — you must still explicitly mark individual todo items completed during execution rather than relying on finish to clean up.
